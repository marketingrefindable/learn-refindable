Recurring themes

"Flapping" — short repeated drops, not a hard outagemost-cited technical pattern

Connection cuts for 20–30 seconds every 15–30 minutes, all day, for weeks. One commenter (Butte, MT) posted about this same pattern roughly eight separate times across two months.

"I found this is called flapping. They cut your internet for 20 to 30 seconds every 20 minutes... I am not paying for this anymore."— Jean Renouard, Butte MT, repeated across ~8 comments
Outage map contradicts lived experiencecredibility gap

Multiple independent commenters, in different states, report a real local outage while the site/Spectrum's own map shows zero problems — the same gap visible in the Plover screenshot you shared (0 signals, 0 direct reports, yet three recent city reports below it).

"NC isn't showing an outage on the map... They couldn't give me an estimated restoration time either."— Annie Oakley / Bryan Goddard thread
No credit or compensation for downtimebilling friction

Recurs independently in at least 6 threads — commenters doing their own math on hours lost vs. monthly bill and asking, rhetorically or directly, for a refund.

"This month 400 hours down. So that's 4800 hours per year... Should most Subscribers do the Same?"— Dale L Sursely, El Paso 79936, x3 comments
"No other option" — monopoly framingstructural complaint

Distinct from generic anger: several commenters specifically frame the issue as captive-market, citing lack of fiber/AT&T/Verizon competition in their area as the reason they tolerate it.

"The worst part is this spectrum company has a monopoly on the internet in my area and I can't even switch to Verizon."— Joe Chain
Switching intent named explicitlychurn signal

Named alternatives, in order of frequency mentioned: Starlink, T-Mobile Home Internet, AT&T, Fidium Fiber, Verizon Fios, Xfinity. Fidium and Starlink mentions specifically cite better reliability, not just price.

Weather blamed, often disbelievedtrust issue

Spectrum reps reportedly cite storms/weather even on clear days; one rep is quoted blaming "the war." Commenters increasingly push back rather than accept the explanation.

"How come Spectrum say it's because of the weather when it's hardly even raining outside... how come wire 3 doesn't have a problem?"— Shirley Dacenzo

Worth flagging

Possible scam / injected content in the thread

The site's own comment guidelines warn: "Beware of 'support numbers' or 'recovery' accounts that might be posted below." One comment in the set (Eric Michael Schmidt, zip 32792) separately describes suspicious "FBI One3" pop-ups appearing during an internet outage — unrelated to Spectrum but worth noting as the kind of thing that shows up in unmoderated outage-comment sections. No actual fake-support-number comment was found in the transcript provided, but the site's own warning plus that one report is reason to treat any phone number or "click here to fix" text in this thread as untrusted if you go read the live page yourself.
